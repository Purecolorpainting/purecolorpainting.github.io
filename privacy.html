Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Pure Color Painting ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through purecolorpainting.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you use the estimate request form on our Site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • The service you are interested in
  • Anything you write in your message, such as your address or project details

We also receive whatever information you choose to share when you call, text, or email us.

Information collected automatically. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about your visit, such as the pages you view, how long you stay, the website that referred you, your general location (city or region), and your device and browser type. We use this only in aggregate to understand how the Site is used.

Like most websites, the services that deliver our Site's fonts and scripts (Google Fonts and jsDelivr) receive your IP address and browser information when your browser loads those files.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your estimate request and answer your questions
  • To schedule, provide, and follow up on painting services
  • To understand how visitors use our Site so we can improve it
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing or advertising.

Service Providers We Use

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Site and our business:

  • EmailJS delivers messages from our contact form to our email inbox.
  • Google provides Google Analytics (only if you accept cookies), Google Fonts, and our email service.
  • jsDelivr delivers a script used by our contact form.
  • Our website hosting provider serves the pages of this Site.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Cookies and Your Choices

Our Site uses Google Analytics cookies (named _ga and _ga_*) only if you click "Accept" on our cookie banner. If you decline, these cookies are not set. We store your choice in your browser so we don't ask again.

You can change your choice at any time:

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a decline. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on (opens in new tab).

How Long We Keep Information

We keep estimate requests and customer correspondence for as long as needed to respond, provide our services, and keep reasonable business records, and then delete them. Google Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting in our Google Analytics account.

How We Protect Information

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as payment card numbers through our contact form.

Your Rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to Other Websites

Our Site links to other websites, such as Facebook and Google Reviews. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
