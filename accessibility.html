Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Pure Color Painting wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use our website and reach us easily.

Our Standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content easier to use for people with a wide range of disabilities.

What We Have Done

  • The site can be navigated with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators and a "Skip to main content" link
  • Images have text descriptions, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers
  • Text and buttons meet minimum color contrast requirements
  • Form fields have labels, and form results are announced to screen readers
  • Pages use headings and landmarks so assistive technology can navigate them
  • Animations are reduced when your device is set to reduce motion
  • Pages can be zoomed up to 200% without losing content

Known Limitations

Some content comes from other companies, such as our Facebook page and Google Reviews. We do not control the accessibility of those websites.
